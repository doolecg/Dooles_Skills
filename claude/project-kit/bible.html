5. How a milestone runs

  1. Start: Claude writes docs/milestone-N-guide.html (goal, parts, out of scope) and asks <Owner> every question it needs in one bundle.
  2. <Owner> approves the guide. Claude marks it "In progress: approved" with the date here and in CLAUDE.md.
  3. Claude builds in small steps. After each step, its test steps go in the guide.
  4. End: one review checklist in the guide for <Owner> (<time budget>). Fixes from it jump the queue.
  5. Close: Claude marks it done here and in CLAUDE.md, ticks its items in section 4, updates docs/systems.md, records decisions in section 3 and docs/design.md, and adds a line to section 8.

6. Where ideas go

  • Fits a planned milestone: add it to that milestone in section 7.
  • Needs something not built yet: park it under the milestone that builds that thing.
  • Serves no pillar: park it under "Maybe never" with one line on why.
  • Changes a settled decision: ask <Owner>; it is not decided until they say so.
  • Never slip it into the milestone in progress, however small.
