# to-make-an-fbx-a-humanoid

Drafted from a working procedure.

1. To make an FBX a Humanoid through UnityMCP, run execute_code with ModelImporter: set animationType to Human and avatarSetup to CreateFromThisModel, then call SaveAndReimport. Check that the avatar loads and is valid, and read the bounds to confirm the scale.
