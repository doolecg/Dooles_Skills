Code antislop is always on via the plugin rules. This covers words and pixels.

## Writing

Say the plain fact. Cut: "stands as a testament", "pivotal", "crucial", "vital", "underscores", "showcases", "vibrant", "tapestry", "delve", "landscape", "seamless", "robust", "leverage", "comprehensive"; "-ing" tails that add nothing ("highlighting...", "ensuring..."); "not just X, it's Y"; reflex lists of three; "experts say" / "many believe"; "serves as" where "is" works; the same noun renamed each sentence.

No em-dash chains, no bold sprinkled through prose, no title-case headings, no emoji, no "**Label:** text" bullet lists, no "Challenges and future outlook", no "In conclusion" or upbeat closer, no "Great question" / "I hope this helps", no knowledge-cutoff disclaimers, no stacked hedging ("might perhaps").

Vary sentence length. Be specific, not general. Have an opinion when asked. Plain words a 16-year-old reads without effort.

## UI and web

One `h1`, headline two lines or fewer at every width, a clear title on the first screen.

Banned: eyebrows or pill badges above headings, gradients, resting shadows or glows, glass or blur panels, hairline cards and thin rules, index numbering (01/02/03), tiny letter-spaced uppercase labels, monospace outside code, icon-topped feature cards or rows of identical cards, emoji, gradient blobs, fake grey-bar dashboards, a styled accent word in a headline, violet/indigo or cream-and-terracotta palettes.

One ground, one ink, one accent. Max two typefaces, usually one. Separate surfaces by a small tone step, not lines. One dominant real image beats six small ones; no placeholder art. Equal outer margins; section gaps clearly bigger than inner gaps. Removing ornament leaves space: fill it with scale, real content or a real picture, never another ornament.

App screens (dashboards, settings, tools) keep the bans but may use dense layouts, tables and form lines.
